Byreki

Privacy Policy

Small-group ministry, thoughtfully organized.

Your ministry information

Byreki stores the information you enter in a workspace on your device. This can include names, contact details, group membership, roles, attendance and absence reasons, care notes, religious milestones, photographs, audio recordings, report files, display preferences, and purchase usage records. Only enter information you are authorized to manage. Sample profiles are illustrative and are marked Sample.

Where information is stored

Workspace records and media are stored in the application’s private storage on your device. Byreki does not provide an account or its own cloud synchronization service. Device backups, device sharing, exported files, and operating-system services may make additional copies under your control. Protect your device and share reports only with appropriate recipients.

Camera, photos, microphone and speech

Camera access is requested when you scan a group invitation or take a profile photo. Photo access is requested when you select a portrait or community mark. Microphone access is requested when you record a care note. Speech recognition is optional and is requested when you tap Transcribe. Apple speech recognition may process recordings using Apple services according to your device settings and Apple’s privacy terms. You can type notes without using these permissions.

Notifications

With your permission, Byreki may schedule local reminders for care follow-ups. Reminder text can appear on your lock screen. You can change notification access in device Settings. Byreki does not send marketing notifications through a remote messaging service.

Purchases and third-party services

Apple processes purchases through the App Store. Byreki does not receive your payment-card information. The app stores verified transaction identifiers, Pass quantities, and usage entries to deliver purchases and prevent duplicate delivery. Third-party services involved in optional actions include Apple’s App Store, speech recognition, system sharing, calling and messaging applications, and GitHub Pages hosting for these legal pages. Those providers may process technical information under their own policies.

Sharing and exports

Reports are generated on your device. A file is shared only through an action you choose in the system share sheet. Calls and messages use the system applications you choose. Group invitation codes select groups in the local workspace; they do not synchronize contacts or attendance between devices. Byreki does not include third-party advertising or behavioral tracking.

Retention and clearing data

Your workspace remains until you remove its records, clear all app data, or remove the application. Clear All Data removes business records, media, generated reports, preferences, and unused Pass balance from this installation. Previously processed purchase transaction identifiers are retained to prevent the same transaction from being granted twice. Exported copies and operating-system backups are outside the app’s deletion controls.

Your choices

You can edit or remove people, update notes and attendance, export selected reports, revoke device permissions, or clear the workspace. Exercise care with information about children and sensitive religious or pastoral matters. The app is intended to be operated by authorized community members and leaders.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the product changes. Effective date: November 4, 2026. Questions about Byreki can be directed to byreki.workspace.help@gmail.com.
