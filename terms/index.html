Using Byreki

Byreki is a device-local workspace for organizing small-group ministry information, gatherings, attendance, member care and reports. Sample content is provided to demonstrate the workspace and does not represent real communication, approval by an organization, or synchronization with a remote church system.

Use the app only for lawful purposes and with appropriate authority to manage the information you enter. Obtain any necessary consent before recording conversations, storing personal or religious information, or sharing a report. Review transcriptions and generated reports for accuracy before relying on or distributing them.

You are responsible for protecting your device and retaining important exports. Byreki does not provide an account-based backup or guaranteed multi-device synchronization. Device loss, uninstallation, storage failure or clearing data may remove information. Permission-dependent features may be unavailable on some devices or when access is declined.

Pass purchases

Pass packs are optional consumable in-app purchases processed by Apple. The App Store displays the final local price before purchase. A report export uses 1 Pass, 3 additional member spaces use 1 Pass, and 10 additional record spaces use 1 Pass. The workspace includes 8 member spaces and 10 record spaces. Filtering and previewing reports are free. A Pass is deducted only after the requested capacity or report result is saved successfully.

Purchase delivery and refunds

Verified purchases are delivered to the local balance. Pending purchases are delivered after Apple confirms them. Consumable balances are associated with this installation and are not presented as a cross-device account entitlement. Refund requests and payment disputes are handled through Apple under its applicable policies. Do not assume a consumed or cleared balance can be restored.

Clearing your workspace

Clear All Data permanently removes local profiles, business records, photos, audio, reports, preferences and unused Passes after confirmation. Export important files first. Minimal processed-transaction identifiers are retained solely to prevent duplicate purchase delivery. Copies that you previously shared are not deleted by this action.
